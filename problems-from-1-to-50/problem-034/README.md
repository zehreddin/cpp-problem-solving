Sales Commission Calculator

A C++ program that calculates sales commission based on the total sales amount.

The commission rate depends on the sales range:

- Sales above 1,000,000: 1%
- Sales above 500,000: 2%
- Sales above 100,000: 3%
- Sales from 50,000 to 100,000 (inclusive): 5%
- Sales below 50,000: 0%

The program takes the total sales amount as input and displays the calculated commission.
